# Monopoly Bank – User guide (English)

Monopoly Bank replaces the paper money of your board game. It runs in the browser, can be installed like an app and works **without internet**.

## 1. Install
1. Open the app's address in your phone's browser (once, with internet).
2. **Android / Chrome:** Settings → *Install app*, or browser menu → *Install app*.
   **iPhone / Safari:** *Share* → *Add to Home Screen*.
3. From now on, start it from your home screen. It works offline.

## 2. Choose a mode
| Mode | When |
|---|---|
| **One shared bank** | One phone or tablet for the whole table. The banker (or whoever's turn it is) enters all payments. |
| **Wallet per player** | Everyone has their own phone. Players pay each other with QR codes. |

**Game edition.** Boxes differ: currency, starting money and GO salary. Pick the closest edition (*Classic M*, *Dollars*, *Pounds*, *Euro*, *Millions* in the Here & Now style), then change anything that differs on your box:
- starting money and GO salary,
- currency symbol and whether it goes before (*M1,500*) or after (*1,500 €*) the amount,
- in shared mode, the *Free Parking jackpot* house rule.

Tap *Save as my edition* to keep your settings under a name such as "Grandma's box". Next time it is in the list. Saved editions stay on your device.

> Why can't the app scan the box? No public catalogue maps a box's barcode to its currency and prices, and looking one up online would send data from your phone. Save your box once as your own edition instead.

## 3. One shared bank
- Every player has a **title deed card** showing their balance.
- **GO +200** – player passed GO.
- **Pay** – money from this player. Choose who receives it (bank, Free Parking, another player).
- **Coin** – drag a player's coin onto another player, the bank or Free Parking: who pays and who receives are already filled in, just type the amount. Tapping the coin works like **Pay**.
- **Get** – money to this player (from the bank by default).
- Enter amounts on the keypad, like at a card terminal. **000** adds three zeros, **×** clears the amount.
- **⋯ → Declare bankruptcy** – the player's remaining money goes to the creditor and the player is out.
- **Undo** – in the green message right after a payment, or in **History**.

## 4. Wallet per player
**Start:** one player taps *Start game* and sets the rules. An **invite QR code** appears (also later via the QR icon at the top).
**Join:** the others tap *Join game* and scan the invite (or type the 16-character game code). You can also scan the invite with the normal phone camera.

On your wallet:
- **Passed GO** – adds the GO salary.
- **From the bank / Pay the bank** – each player enters their own payments with the bank.
- **Pay a player** – enter the amount, then show the QR code to the other player. The money leaves your wallet immediately.
- **Receive** – scan the other player's QR code (or type its 12-character code).

Every payment code works **only once** and **only in your game**. If the other player has not scanned it yet, you can cancel it (History → *Code* → *Cancel payment*).

## 5. Good to know
- The game is saved automatically. Close the app any time and continue later.
- *Settings → End game* deletes the game from this device.
- *Keep screen on* stops the screen from turning off during a game.
- When a new version is available, the app shows *Update*.
- The camera needs permission. Without a camera, type the codes instead.

## 6. Privacy
No account, no tracking. Players, balances and transactions **never leave your device**. When online, the app sends one empty "ping" when it starts so the author can see how often it is used. It contains no game data.
